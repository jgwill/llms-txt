# Anti-Patterns and Self-Correction for LLMs {#anti-patterns}

> Eight misapplications recur when LLMs apply structural thinking. Each one imports a model or an orientation the method excludes. When one appears in your own output, pause, return to Step 1, and rebuild the picture from what was actually said.

**Misinterpretation risks:** this list is a check on your own output, not a vocabulary to apply to the person you are working with. Telling someone "you are using willpower language" is its own departure from the method.

## The Eight Anti-Patterns {#eight}

1. **Importing external frameworks.** Start with Nothing means no root-cause analysis, no diagnostic models, no "this is similar to…". Each situation is unique.

2. **Framing situations as problems.** "The problem is…" switches on problem-solving orientation. Say "What we observe is…" or "The current reality is…".

3. **Gap-filling language.** "Bridge the gap", "fill the void", "close the distance" describe emptiness. Structural tension is an active force, not a gap to fill ([Structural Tension](./llms-st-structural-tension.md#language)).

4. **Prescribing before understanding.** Understanding comes first. Solving before understanding biases what you can see.

5. **Confusing oscillation with failure.** Oscillation is a structural pattern, not a moral judgment. It is information about the structure ([Patterns](./llms-st-patterns.md)).

6. **Willpower and force language.** "Push through", "strategies to overcome", "determination" signal reactive orientation. The creative process works through structural tension, not force.

7. **Comparing to best practices.** Importing an external model violates Start with Nothing. Ground in the information provided.

8. **Hypothesis-driven approaches.** Root-cause analysis and hypothesis testing import models. Structural thinking observes what is, without theory.

A related habit belongs with these: **adding value words to current reality** ("honest current reality", "an honest assessment"). Current reality is what is. The qualifier performs a value without carrying information, and a description distorted by concepts is corrected by observing again, not by calling it honest.

## Self-Correction Method {#self-correction}

When you detect reactive language in your own output:

1. **Pause.** Notice the reactive pattern.
2. **Return to Step 1.** Start with Nothing; release the preconception.
3. **Re-picture.** What did the person actually say? Build the picture fresh.
4. **Re-question.** Generate questions only from the picture, not from external models.
5. **Check the pattern.** Is what you describe oscillating or resolving/advancing?
