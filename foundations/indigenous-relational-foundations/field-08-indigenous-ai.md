# Field 08 — Indigenous AI: The Standard Already Set

**Sources:** `lewis-etal-2020`, `lewis-whaanga-yolgormez-2024`

---

## Why this field is in the packet

This portfolio claims a place within an existing research field. `docs/medicine-wheel-research.md` states that the Medicine Wheel Developer Suite "operationalizes Abundant Intelligences principles as code" and positions the work "within funded, multi-institutional research (Haudenosaunee Pod, Ka Hawai'i Pae 'āina Pod, etc.)."

That field has published standards and a defining structure. Both are verifiable, and both set the bar this work will be measured against.

---

## The two reference documents

**Lewis, J.E. (ed.), Abdilla, A., Arista, N., et al. (2020).** *Indigenous Protocol and Artificial Intelligence Position Paper.* Initiative for Indigenous Futures & CIFAR. DOI 10.11573/spectrum.library.concordia.ca.00986506.

A collection of design guidelines, scholarly essays, artworks, creative writing, and prototype descriptions articulating Indigenous Knowledge Systems in conversation with AI. **This is the appropriate external standard against which the Medicine Wheel suite should be assessed** — and the assessment should be done, not assumed.

Its most instructive feature is structural: many named Indigenous authors, speaking from named nations, in their own voices, with disagreement preserved rather than harmonised. That is the form the field has established for this kind of work.

**Lewis, J.E., Whaanga, H. & Yolgörmez, C. (2024).** "Abundant intelligences: placing AI within Indigenous knowledge frameworks." *AI & SOCIETY* 40(4), 2141–2157. DOI 10.1007/s00146-024-02099-4.

The peer-reviewed statement of the Abundant Intelligences programme this portfolio claims alignment with. Verified programme facts:

- Indigenous-led, **Indigenous-majority** international interdisciplinary team
- **37 co-investigators and collaborators**, majority Indigenous researchers and community members
- **8 universities and 12 Indigenous community-based organizations** across Canada, the United States and Aotearoa New Zealand
- New Frontiers in Research Fund Transformation grant (~CAD 23M), **1 March 2023 – 28 February 2029**
- Organised in locally rooted **"pods"** working alongside Indigenous knowledge keepers

---

## What "alignment" means in this field

The programme's defining feature is not its topic. It is its **constitution**: Indigenous leadership, Indigenous majority, community-based organizations as partners, knowledge keepers in the room.

"Alignment with Abundant Intelligences" is therefore a claim about **relationships and governance**, not about subject-matter overlap. A project can share every research interest with the programme and still not be aligned with it, if it is not constituted the way the programme is constituted.

This repository currently has: no named Indigenous co-investigator, no partner community-based organization, no knowledge keeper in the loop (the `LICENSE` records them as "to be designated"), and no recorded community review. On the field's own criteria, "alignment" is not currently an accurate description.

**"Informed by" or "in conversation with" would be accurate.** "Aligned with" reads as a claim to participation the work has not yet established, and a reader familiar with the programme will notice.

---

## What this field offers the portfolio

This is not only a constraint. It is the clearest available path forward, and it is genuinely open:

1. **A published standard to assess against.** Running the Medicine Wheel suite against the Indigenous Protocol and AI design guidelines would be a real, citable, honest piece of work — and would produce findings worth publishing whichever way they came out.
2. **A model for authorship.** The 2020 position paper's form — many named voices from named nations, disagreement preserved — is directly applicable to a portfolio that already practises "polyphonic design review" (`llms-polyphonic-design-review.md`). The polyphonic method is a genuine strength here; it currently has AI personas where the field has people.
3. **A demonstrated route to relationship.** The pod model shows what community partnership looks like in practice in exactly this domain.

The technical work in this repository is substantial. What it lacks relative to this field is not sophistication — it is people. That is addressable, but not by writing.

## Engineering implications

1. **Change "aligned with" to "informed by"** in `docs/medicine-wheel-research.md` regarding Abundant Intelligences, unless a formal relationship exists that is not recorded in this repository.
2. **Cite Lewis, Whaanga & Yolgörmez (2024)**, DOI 10.1007/s00146-024-02099-4, wherever Abundant Intelligences is invoked. It is currently referenced by programme URL only.
3. **Cite the Indigenous Protocol and AI Position Paper (2020)**, DOI 10.11573/spectrum.library.concordia.ca.00986506, in `docs/ceremonial-technology.md`.
4. **Consider a formal self-assessment** of the `@medicine-wheel/*` suite against the position paper's design guidelines, published in `foundations/` as a follow-on packet. This would be the single most credibility-building artifact the project could produce, and it requires no permission from anyone.
5. **Remove or substantiate the specific pod references.** Naming the "Haudenosaunee Pod" and "Ka Hawai'i Pae 'āina Pod" in proximity to a claim of alignment implies a connection to those pods. If none exists, the reference should be reframed as describing the programme rather than situating this work within it.
